The STAR App

Support Tool for Autonomy and Reflection

STAR is being developed as one connected place for individual participation, team communication, and support documentation. Individuals can share preferences and reflect on experiences, while families and caregivers coordinate, record daily support, and review patterns. Together, the person’s input and the information gathered over time support more informed conversations about goals, leisure, activities, and everyday decisions.

  • Support individual input through pictures, simple choices, emoji responses, and read-aloud tools.
  • Bring the individual’s preferences into person-centered goal planning and activity choices.
  • Keep the support team connected through shared communication.
  • Document daily health, routines, participation, services, who provided support, and where.
  • Bring reports and patterns alongside the individual’s own input to inform decisions together.

Meet the founder

Built from life.
Built with care.

I’m Beth Adams, founder of North Star Solutions Tech in Chester, Vermont, and mom to Benny. STAR is a passion project because I want Benny—and others who need communication support—to have a meaningful say in their own lives.

Person-centered planning means the individual’s goals, interests, and preferences guide the conversation. When expressing choices or advocating for yourself is difficult, that takes thoughtful support. I’m building STAR for individuals as well as caregivers, so people can share what they enjoy and help shape goals, leisure, activities, and everyday decisions.

Informed decisions also need a clear picture of everyday life. That’s why STAR brings team communication, daily documentation, and reports together in one place. The individual’s voice and the information gathered over time belong side by side, helping everyone understand what’s happening and plan support together.

Action! grew from another part of life with Benny. When he was eight, we discovered his gift for remembering movie scripts and began making personalized home videos to help him practice skills and communicate. That experience inspired an app where individuals can direct, star in, and watch their own skills movies.

Through my woman-owned, woman-led business, I’m creating tools for connection, choice, and participation.

Beth AdamsFounder & OwnerNorth Star Solutions Tech · Chester, Vermont
