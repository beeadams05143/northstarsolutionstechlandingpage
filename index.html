Meet the founder

Built from life.
Built with care.

I’m Elizabeth Katherine Adams, but most people call me Beth. I created STAR from my experience as a mom and caregiver to my adult son, Benny. When several people share in someone’s care, important details can get scattered across conversations, notes, and memory. I wanted a clearer way to bring those pieces together while keeping Benny’s voice, preferences, and everyday experiences at the center. Through North Star Solutions Tech, my woman-owned and woman-led small business in Chester, Vermont, I’m building practical tools for families and care teams navigating similar challenges. My hope is simple: less time piecing information together, more understanding, and more room for the person behind every check-in.
